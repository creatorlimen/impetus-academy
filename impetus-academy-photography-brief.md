# Impetus Academy Photography Brief

Internal working document. Do not publish this file or its approval notes as website copy.

## Purpose

The website now reserves intentional media spaces for approved school photography. Images should show a warm, credible learning community while giving Primary School and Secondary School equal visual quality, quantity, and prominence.

Temporary website fallbacks are abstract brand illustrations. Do not replace them with stock photographs or photorealistic AI-generated students.

## Approval and consent

Before an image is published:

- Confirm that the school owns the image or has permission to publish it.
- Confirm that every identifiable student has appropriate publication consent.
- Confirm whether the image belongs to Primary, Secondary, leadership, facilities, or mixed-community use.
- Avoid images containing private documents, visible student records, financial information, or sensitive personal data.
- Record the approval status outside public website content.
- Do not infer consent because an image previously appeared on WordPress or social media.

## Required image groups

### 1. Homepage Primary hero

- Subject: Primary students actively learning, reading, creating, or collaborating.
- Orientation: wide landscape.
- Preferred minimum: 2400 × 1600 pixels.
- Composition: leave calm space on the left or around the edges for responsive cropping.
- Avoid: posed line-ups, cluttered backgrounds, illegible worksheets, or a single child dominating the frame without approval.

### 2. Homepage Secondary hero

- Subject: Junior or Senior Secondary students engaged in study, practical work, discussion, science, technology, commerce, or creative activity.
- Orientation: wide landscape.
- Preferred minimum: 2400 × 1600 pixels.
- Match the Primary hero in image quality, energy, and prominence.

### 3. Primary overview

- Subject: a warm classroom or activity moment showing strong foundations, participation, and care.
- Orientation: landscape, approximately 16:10.
- Preferred minimum: 1800 × 1125 pixels.

### 4. Secondary overview

- Subject: collaborative or independent learning with visible subject depth.
- Orientation: landscape, approximately 16:10.
- Preferred minimum: 1800 × 1125 pixels.
- Match the Primary overview image in treatment and quality.

### 5. Founder portrait

- Subject: approved founder portrait.
- Orientation: vertical, approximately 4:5.
- Preferred minimum: 1600 × 2000 pixels.
- Background: uncluttered, with enough space for flexible cropping.
- Confirm preferred public name and title before pairing the portrait with final copy.

### 6. Proprietor portrait

- Subject: approved proprietor portrait.
- Orientation: vertical, approximately 4:5.
- Preferred minimum: 1600 × 2000 pixels.
- Match the founder portrait in quality and formality.
- Confirm preferred title and biography before publication.

### 7. Life at Impetus

Collect a balanced set covering:

- Classroom learning.
- Reading and writing.
- Mathematics or science.
- Art or creative work.
- Practical or technology-supported learning.
- Group discussion.
- School community moments.
- Approved events, clubs, sports, or activities when verified.

Use a mix of landscape, portrait, and detail photographs.

### 8. Gallery

- Supply original-resolution files rather than screenshots or compressed messaging-app previews.
- Aim for a balanced Primary/Secondary collection.
- Include wide establishing images, medium activity images, and close detail images.
- Avoid publishing multiple near-identical frames.

## File naming

Use descriptive filenames without student names:

- primary-reading-group-01.jpg
- secondary-science-activity-01.jpg
- community-event-01.jpg
- founder-portrait-approved.jpg

Do not use names such as IMG_0042.jpg in the final website asset collection.

## Delivery notes

For each image, provide:

- Division or category.
- Short description.
- Publication approval status.
- Photographer or source when known.
- Any cropping restrictions.
- Names or private information that must not be published.

The final integration pass will optimize images, set focal points, provide responsive sizes, write appropriate alt text, and verify mobile and desktop crops.
